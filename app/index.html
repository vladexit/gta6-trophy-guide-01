Roadmap to the Platinum

Platinum: “Vice Got a Price!” — about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10. Every trophy is single-player; there are no online trophies and no DLC trophies at launch.

  1. Play the story first. Finish all five chapters and the final mission. Story trophies pop as you go, and the story unlocks stranger missions, activities and map areas.
  2. Clear the stranger strands. Follow the “?” markers (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret, Cal) and finish each strand — including the Silver for 8 strands.
  3. Work the activities. Shooting Range golds, drone races, card sets, WAiNK It spots, meditation spots and the 100-vehicle discovery run.
  4. Grind the crime trophies. Bank vaults, armored trucks, shop robberies with your partner, Marked Bills laundering.
  5. Cleanup. 250 stunts, 50 challenges, the 100% completion checklist (35 tasks) for the Gold “A Long Road” — then the Platinum pops.
Missables & warnings. “Positive Mental Attitude” (meditate at every spot) is the one possible missable — do it as you explore and don’t rush past meditation spots. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of each chapter. Cheats disable trophies and saving — never turn cheats on in a trophy run.
